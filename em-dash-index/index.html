By lab

Average em dashes per launch post.

Method. Each post's article body is downloaded and its U+2014 characters counted by script; navigation, footers and cookie banners are excluded. En dashes (–) and hyphens don't count. Dashes inside customer quotes and table cells do, including the lone dashes that stand for empty cells.

What's tracked. Model launch posts on each lab's own site since January 2023, found from the lab's feed or sitemap. Coverage of older posts is uneven: Google's before December 2024 and Meta's Llama 3 and 4 were published elsewhere and aren't here. Labs without launch posts on their own site (Qwen, MiniMax, Z.ai) aren't included.

Updates. A script checks every lab once a day and adds new launch posts. Posts from the last three weeks are recounted on each run, since launch posts get edited after they go up.

Per 1,000 words. Posts under 500 words are marked short; one dash moves their rate a long way. xAI's pages have no article tag, so their word counts include some page chrome.
